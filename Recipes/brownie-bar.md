Makes 6 bars, a whole batch: one bar each is a serving, so a batch covers three
pudding Slots. Macros are per bar. Keeps 5–7 days in the fridge.

1. Mash the banana in a bowl, then stir in the almond milk and a teaspoon of honey.
2. Mix in the oats, protein powder, cocoa and baking powder until no dry pockets remain, then fold through the chocolate chips.
3. Spread into a lined tin that fits the air fryer basket and bake at 160°C for 25–30 minutes, until a skewer comes out with just a few crumbs.
4. Cool in the tin, cut into 6 bars and keep in the fridge.
