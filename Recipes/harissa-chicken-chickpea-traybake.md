  - { ingredient: red-pepper, qty: 2, unit: each, prep: cut into chunks }
  - { ingredient: red-onion, qty: 1, unit: each, prep: cut into wedges }
  - { ingredient: harissa-paste, qty: 30, unit: g }
  - { ingredient: olive-oil, qty: 10, unit: g }

  - { ingredient: greek-yogurt, qty: 100, unit: g }

  - { ingredient: feta, qty: 60, unit: g, prep: crumbled }
  - { ingredient: salt }
---

1. Heat the oven to 200°C fan. Toss the chickpeas, pepper and onion in a roasting tin with the oil and a third of the harissa, and season.
2. Rub the chicken with the rest of the harissa and sit it on top of the veg. Roast for 25 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through and the chickpeas are starting to crisp.
3. Lift out the chicken and stir the quinoa and spinach through the hot tin, then put it back in the oven for 2 minutes, until the spinach wilts and the quinoa is warmed through.
4. Mix the yogurt with the lemon juice and a pinch of salt. Slice the chicken and serve it over the chickpeas with the yogurt drizzled over and the feta crumbled on top.
