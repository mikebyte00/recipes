1. Boil the wholewheat linguine in salted water until tender, then drain (reserve a splash of pasta water).
2. Toss the courgette slices onto the Ninja Sizzle with the olive oil and a pinch of salt. Grill for 4–5 minutes until caramelized and tender. Toss the pine nuts onto the plate for the last 60 seconds to lightly toast, then set the veg and nuts aside.
3. Melt the butter on the hob with the lemon juice, half the lemon zest, and the dried herbs. Lay the white fish fillets directly into the lemon butter and cook for 2–3 minutes per side until it flakes effortlessly.
4. Toss the hot linguine, roasted courgette, and toasted pine nuts directly into the remaining lemon-butter juices (add a splash of pasta water if it needs loosening). Top with the flaky fish and finish with the remaining lemon zest.
