  - { ingredient: cottage-cheese, qty: 120, unit: g }

1. Tip the diced tofu straight into a hot skillet with the olive oil and gochujang paste. Stir-fry for 5 minutes until golden and coated in the glossy paste.
2. Add the drained cannellini beans and sweetcorn. Toss for 1–2 minutes until steaming hot.
3. Remove from the heat completely and stir through the cottage cheese to create a spicy, creamy, comforting sauce.
