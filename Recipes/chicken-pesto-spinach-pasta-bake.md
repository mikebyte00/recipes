1. Heat the oven to 200°C fan. Toss the chicken with the oil and some salt and pepper in an ovenproof dish, and roast for 15 minutes.
2. Meanwhile, cook the penne in a pot of salted water as per the pack instructions, adding the spinach for the last 30 seconds. Drain, keeping a splash of the water.
3. Stir the pasta and spinach, pesto, tomatoes and the splash of water into the dish with the chicken. Tear the mozzarella over and scatter with the parmesan.
4. Bake for 12–15 minutes, until golden and bubbling.
