1. Fry the tofu in a hot skillet with olive oil and curry powder for 5–6 minutes until cooked through and coated in the spices.
2. Pour in the drained butter beans and baby spinach with a small splash of water. Toss for 2 minutes until the spinach wilts and the beans are hot.
3. Remove from the heat completely and stir through the Greek yogurt to create a creamy sauce.
