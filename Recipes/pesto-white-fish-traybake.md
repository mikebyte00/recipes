1. Heat the oven to 200°C fan. Toss the potatoes in a roasting tin with the oil and some salt and pepper, and roast for 20 minutes.
2. Add the green beans, cannellini beans and tomatoes, toss with half the pesto and roast for 5 minutes.
3. Sit the fish skin-side down on top and spread the rest of the pesto over the flesh. Lay a few lemon slices alongside and roast for 12–15 minutes, until the fish flakes easily.
4. Serve with the rest of the lemon squeezed over. The skin lifts off on the plate if you'd rather not eat it.
