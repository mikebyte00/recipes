  - { ingredient: oil-spray, note: light spray, staple: true }
  - { ingredient: salt, qty: 1, unit: pinch, staple: true }
  - { ingredient: black-pepper, qty: 1, unit: pinch, staple: true }
---

1. Slice the sausages into chunks.
2. Toss them and the drained chickpeas directly into your air fryer basket with a light oil spray, salt, and pepper.
3. Air fry at 200°C for 10–12 minutes, shaking halfway, until the sausages are golden and the chickpeas are beautifully crispy.
4. **To Serve:** Tip into a bowl and stir through cottage cheese.
