1. Chop the tofu into bite-sized pieces.
2. Toss the tofu and drained chickpeas directly in a bowl with harissa paste, lemon juice, and a light oil spray.
3. Tip everything into the air fryer basket and cook at 190°C for 12–14 minutes, shaking halfway, until the tofu is cooked through and the chickpeas are crisp.
4. **To Serve:** Top with a dollop of cold Greek yogurt.
