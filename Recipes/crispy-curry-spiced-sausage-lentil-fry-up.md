  - { ingredient: lentils, qty: 1, unit: pack, note: pre-cooked }
  - { ingredient: curry-powder, qty: 1, unit: tsp }
  - { ingredient: cottage-cheese, qty: 160, unit: g, note: to serve }
  - { ingredient: oil-spray, note: light spray, staple: true }
---

1. Toss the sliced sausages and lentils with the oil spray and curry powder directly in a bowl or air fryer basket.
2. Air fry at 200°C for 10–12 minutes, shaking halfway through, until the lentils are crispy and the sausages are browned.
3. **To Serve:** Divide into bowls and swirl through the cottage cheese.
