  - { ingredient: brown-rice, qty: 120, unit: g, note: raw }
  - { ingredient: butternut-squash, qty: 250, unit: g, prep: diced, note: frozen is fine }
  - { ingredient: chickpeas, qty: 1, unit: can, prep: drained }

  - { ingredient: spinach, qty: 100, unit: g }

---

1. Add the brown rice and water to the rice cooker and run a standard cycle.
2. Season the tofu with black pepper and air-fry at 200°C for 12–15 minutes, shaking halfway, until golden.
3. Meanwhile, put the squash and garlic in the skillet with a splash of water. Cover and cook over a medium heat for 8–10 minutes until the squash starts to soften.
4. Stir in the vinegar, soy sauce, coconut milk and chickpeas, then simmer gently for 10 minutes until the squash is tender and the sauce is syrupy.
5. Fold in the tofu and spinach for 2 minutes until the spinach wilts, then serve over the rice.
