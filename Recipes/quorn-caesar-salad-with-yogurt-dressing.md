1. Lower the eggs into a pot of boiling water and cook for 7 minutes, then cool them under the cold tap.
2. Meanwhile, air-fry the Quorn pieces and bread cubes at 200°C for 8–10 minutes, shaking halfway, until the Quorn is golden and the croutons are crisp.
3. Stir the yogurt, half the parmesan, the lemon juice and plenty of black pepper together to make the dressing.
4. Toss the lettuce with the dressing and pile it into bowls. Top with the Quorn, croutons, the halved eggs and the rest of the parmesan.
