1. Mix the honey, sriracha, garlic and half the soy sauce in a bowl. Turn the salmon in it to coat and leave for 10 minutes.
2. Air-fry the salmon at 200°C for 10–12 minutes, spooning over the leftover marinade halfway, until it flakes easily.
3. Heat the sesame oil on the Ninja Sizzle. Stir-fry the onion, pepper, baby corn, tenderstem and edamame for 5–6 minutes until charred at the edges but still crisp.
4. Add the rice noodles and the remaining soy sauce and toss for 1–2 minutes until hot through.
5. Serve the salmon over the noodles and stir-fry veg, scattered with the sesame seeds.
