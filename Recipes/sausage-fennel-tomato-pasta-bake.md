1. Heat the oven to 200°C fan and put a pot of salted water on for the pasta. Heat the oil in the skillet and soften the onion for 5 minutes. Squeeze the sausagemeat into the pan, break it up and fry for 8 minutes until browned, then add the fennel seeds and garlic for 1 minute.
2. Pour in the passata and simmer for 5 minutes. Meanwhile, cook the penne for 2 minutes less than the pack says, then drain.
3. Stir the pasta into the sauce and tip it into an ovenproof dish. Tear the mozzarella over and scatter with the parmesan.
4. Bake for 15 minutes, until bubbling and golden.
