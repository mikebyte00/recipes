1. Warm the drained beans in the corner of your skillet with tomato purée, smoked paprika, Worcestershire sauce, honey, and a splash of water for 2 minutes until thick and glossy.
2. Toast your bread.
3. Mash the avocado and spread it on top of the bread.
4. Fry your eggs alongside.
5. **To Serve:** Serve over toasted sourdough with mashed avocado.
