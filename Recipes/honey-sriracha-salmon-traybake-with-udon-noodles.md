1. Heat the oven to 200°C fan. Mix the honey, sriracha, garlic and a third of the soy sauce in a bowl. Turn the salmon in it to coat, then cover and chill until needed.
2. Tip the stir-fry veg into a roasting tin with the rest of the soy sauce and the sesame oil. Toss together and roast for 5 minutes.
3. Stir the udon noodles and 3 tbsp water into the tin. Sit the salmon on top, spoon over any leftover marinade and scatter everything with the sesame seeds.
4. Roast for 12–15 minutes, until the salmon flakes easily. Serve straight away.
