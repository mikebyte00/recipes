1. Heat the oven to 200°C fan. Mix the honey, sriracha, garlic and a third of the soy sauce in a bowl. Turn the salmon in it to coat, then cover and chill until needed.
2. Tip the broccoli, edamame, baby corn, pepper and onion into a roasting tin with the rest of the soy sauce and the sesame oil. Toss together and roast for 5 minutes.
3. Stir the udon noodles and 3 tbsp water into the tin. Sit the salmon on top, skin-side down, spoon over any leftover marinade and scatter everything with the sesame seeds.
4. Roast for 12–15 minutes, until the salmon flakes easily. Serve straight away; the skin lifts off on the plate if you'd rather not eat it.
