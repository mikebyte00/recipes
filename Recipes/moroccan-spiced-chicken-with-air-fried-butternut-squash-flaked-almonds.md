title: Moroccan Spiced Chicken with Air-Fried Butternut Squash & Flaked Almonds
slot: dinner
protein: chicken
effort: medium
tags: []
appliances: [air-fryer]
macros: { protein_g: 48, kcal: 730 }
ingredients:
  - { ingredient: chicken-thighs, qty: 4, unit: each, note: "skin on, bone out" }
  - { ingredient: butternut-squash, qty: 450, unit: g, prep: diced }
  - { ingredient: courgette, qty: 150, unit: g, prep: sliced into half-moons }
  - { ingredient: ras-el-hanout, qty: 15, unit: g }
  - { ingredient: flaked-almonds, qty: 15, unit: g }
  - { ingredient: olive-oil }
  - { ingredient: salt, qty: 1, unit: pinch }
---

1. Toss the butternut squash cubes and sliced courgette in a bowl with olive oil, salt, and half the Moroccan spice blend.
2. Tip into the air fryer and cook at 200°C for 15 minutes, tossing halfway through, until the squash is tender and caramelized at the edges. Scatter the flaked almonds in for the final 2 minutes to toast.
3. Rub the remaining spice blend onto the chicken thighs and cook in the air fryer for 12 minutes. Serve over the roasted squash base.
