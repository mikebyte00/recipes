1. Toss the halved baby potatoes with 5g olive oil, sea salt, and dried parsley. Air-fry at 200°C for 18 minutes until golden and crispy-edged.
2. Heat the remaining oil on the Ninja Sizzle plate. Char the green beans for 4 minutes, then push to the side. Sear the salmon fillets skin-side down for 4 minutes, flip for 2 minutes, then remove the fish and beans to a plate.
3. Add the minced garlic to the sizzle plate for 30 seconds. Pour in the stock and Dijon mustard, letting it bubble for 1 minute. Whisk in the light cream cheese and fresh tarragon until smooth and creamy.
4. **To Serve:** Plate the crispy air-fried potatoes alongside the charred green beans, lay the seared salmon on top, and spoon the warm tarragon-Dijon cream sauce over everything.
