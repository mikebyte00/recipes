1. Whisk the peanut butter and honey together until smooth, loosening with a teaspoon of warm water if it's too thick.
2. In a separate bowl, whisk the Greek yogurt with a pinch of cinnamon until light and airy.
3. Fold the peanut butter mixture through the yogurt, leaving a visible ripple rather than blending it smooth.
4. Divide into bowls, top with the sliced banana, and finish with a final dusting of cinnamon.
