1. Whisk the eggs with the milk, salt and pepper.
2. Soak the bread on each side.
3. Add oil to the skillet and once hot carefully place the bread in.
4. Turn after a few minutes to ensure both sides of the bread are cooked.
5. **To Serve:** Serve immediately with salt and pepper.
