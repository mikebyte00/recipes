Makes 9 cookies: quantities are for the whole batch, macros per cookie. Keeps
4–5 days in an airtight tin.

1. Mash the bananas in a bowl, then stir in the peanut butter and oat milk.
2. Fold in the protein powder, oats, hemp seeds and baking powder, then the chopped chocolate.
3. Scoop 9 portions onto lined air fryer trays, flatten slightly, and bake at 180°C for 10–15 minutes until golden.
