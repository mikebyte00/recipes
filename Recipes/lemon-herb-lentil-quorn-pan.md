ingredients:
  - { ingredient: quorn-pieces, qty: 300, unit: g }
  - { ingredient: lentils, qty: 250, unit: g, note: pre-cooked }
  - { ingredient: lemon, qty: 0.5, unit: each, prep: juiced }
  - { ingredient: parsley, qty: 20, unit: g, prep: chopped }
---

1. Sear the quorn pieces in a skillet until golden and piping hot.
2. Push it to one side of the pan and pour the pre-cooked lentils into the other side.
3. Add a splash of water, a squeeze of fresh lemon juice, and chopped fresh parsley.
4. Toss together until hot.
