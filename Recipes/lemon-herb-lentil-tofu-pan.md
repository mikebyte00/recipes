1. Sear the tofu in a skillet until golden on both sides.
2. Push it to one side of the pan and pour the pre-cooked lentils into the other side.
3. Add a splash of water, a squeeze of fresh lemon juice, and chopped fresh parsley.
4. Toss together until hot.
