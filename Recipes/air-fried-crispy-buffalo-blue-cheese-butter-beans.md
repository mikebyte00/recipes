  - { ingredient: blue-cheese, qty: 40, unit: g, prep: crumbled, note: Saint Agur light }
  - { ingredient: greek-yogurt, qty: 80, unit: g }
  - { ingredient: celery, qty: 100, unit: g, prep: finely diced }
  - { ingredient: oil-spray }
---

1. Toss the chicken directly in a bowl with the buffalo sauce and light oil spray.
2. Tip into the air fryer basket and cook at 190°C for 12–14 minutes, shaking halfway, until crispy and cooked through.
3. Warm the drained butter beans in a small skillet for 1 minute. Divide between bowls, top with the crispy buffalo chicken, fold through the diced celery, and drizzle generously with the blue cheese yogurt sauce.
