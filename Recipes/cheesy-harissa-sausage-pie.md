1. This cooks the card's whole batch — 6 portions, three dinners for two — in a 22 × 18cm (1.2 litre) ovenproof dish. The list above is one dinner's share; the batch uses 8 sausages, 1 onion, ½ tbsp light olive oil, 1 tbsp fennel seeds, the whole 350g jar of sauce, both 450g packs of mash, 3–4 tbsp oat milk, 10g parmesan and both 225g packs of greens.
2. Heat the oil in the skillet over a low heat, add the onion and fry for 5 minutes until softening. Squeeze the sausagemeat into the pan, then turn the heat to medium-high. Break it up with a wooden spoon and fry for 8–10 minutes until golden brown. Add the fennel seeds and cook for 2 minutes. Pour in the sauce, then fill a quarter of the jar with water, shake and pour into the pan. Cook for 2 minutes.
3. Heat the grill to its highest setting. Tip the sausage mix into the dish. Heat the mash in the pot over a low heat, stirring, until piping hot (in place of the card's microwave), then loosen with the oat milk and spoon on top of the filling.
4. Top the mash with the parmesan, then grill for 3–5 minutes until golden and bubbling. Let it stand for 5 minutes while you heat tonight's share of the greens as per the pack instructions.
5. Serve two portions. Cool the rest, cover and keep in the fridge. To reheat, cover with foil and bake at 180°C fan for 20–25 minutes until piping hot all the way through, uncovering for the last 5 minutes to crisp the top; heat that night's share of the greens alongside.
