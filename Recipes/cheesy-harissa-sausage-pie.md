1. Heat the oil in the skillet over a low heat, add the onion and fry for 5 minutes until softening. Squeeze the sausagemeat into the pan, then turn the heat to medium-high. Break it up with a wooden spoon and fry for 8–10 minutes until golden brown. Add the fennel seeds and cook for 2 minutes. Pour in the sauce, add about 2 tbsp water and cook for 2 minutes.
2. Heat the grill to its highest setting. Tip the sausage mix into a small ovenproof dish. Heat the mash in the pot over a low heat, stirring, until piping hot (in place of the card's microwave), then loosen with the oat milk and spoon on top of the filling.
3. Top the mash with the parmesan, then grill for 3–5 minutes until golden and bubbling. Let it stand for 5 minutes while you heat the greens as per the pack instructions.
