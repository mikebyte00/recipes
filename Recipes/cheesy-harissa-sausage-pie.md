1. Boil the potatoes in the pot for 15 minutes until tender, dropping in the peas for the final 3 minutes. Lift out the peas, drain the potatoes, and mash them with the Greek yogurt and half the parmesan.
2. Meanwhile, heat the olive oil in the skillet and soften the onion for 5 minutes. Add the sausage and fry for 5–6 minutes, breaking it up, until golden.
3. Stir in the harissa, passata, olives and lentils and simmer for 5 minutes until thick.
4. Tip the filling into a dish that fits the air fryer, spoon the mash over and scatter with the remaining parmesan. Air-fry at 200°C for 8–10 minutes until golden and bubbling.
5. Serve with the peas.
