  - { ingredient: cherry-tomatoes, qty: 150, unit: g, prep: halved }
  - { ingredient: lemon, qty: 0.5, unit: each, prep: juiced }
  - { ingredient: feta, qty: 60, unit: g, note: lighter }
  - { ingredient: olive-oil }
---

1. Cut the tofu into pieces.
2. Fry the tofu until cooked through.
3. Toss the drained chickpeas and halved cherry tomatoes straight into the hot pan with the tofu.
4. Cook just for 2 minutes until the tomatoes begin to soften and release their juices.
5. Toss with lemon juice, pull off the heat, and crumble over the feta.
