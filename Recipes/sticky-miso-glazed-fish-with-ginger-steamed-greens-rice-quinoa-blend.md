1. Add the wholegrain rice into the rice cooker with water (1:2 ratio), grated ginger, and a pinch of salt.
2. Whisk the miso paste, honey, soy sauce, and sesame oil together. Coat your fish fillets generously.
3. Preheat the sizzle plate and char the pak choi.
4. Air-fry the fish at 190°C for 8–10 minutes until the miso glaze is bubbling and golden. Serve directly over the ginger-scented grain and greens base.
