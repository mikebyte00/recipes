  - { ingredient: oil-spray, qty: 4, unit: g, staple: true }
  - { ingredient: dried-italian-herbs, qty: 3, unit: g, note: source reads "Italian herbs or garlic powder", staple: true }
  - { ingredient: cottage-cheese, qty: 160, unit: g, note: to serve }
---

1. Toss the sausage chunks, cannellini beans, oil spray, and herb seasoning together.
2. Air fry at 200°C for 10–12 minutes, shaking halfway, until the beans get a nice outer crunch and the sausage edges turn golden.
3. **To Serve:** Serve immediately over cold cottage cheese for a hot-cold contrast.
