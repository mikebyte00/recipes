1. Marinate the chicken breasts in the Peri-Peri sauce and olive oil for at least 30 minutes.
2. Place the chicken breasts and waffle fries in the air fryer. Cook at 200°C for 15–18 minutes. Brush the chicken with extra Peri-Peri sauce during the last 3 minutes of cooking.
3. Lightly toast your buns.
4. Lightly grill the halloumi and pineapple on the Ninja Sizzle.
5. Spread the spicy and garlic mayo on each half of the bun. Layer with lettuce, halloumi, pineapple, red onion, and the peri-peri chicken breast.
6. **To Serve:** Serve immediately with the side of spiced waffle fries.
