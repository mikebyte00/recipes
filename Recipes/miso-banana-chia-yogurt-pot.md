  - { ingredient: greek-yogurt, qty: 400, unit: g }

---

1. Whisk the chia seeds into the oat milk and kefir, then chill for at least 2 hours, or overnight, until it sets into a thick pudding.
2. Mash the bananas with the miso paste until smooth.
3. Layer into two glasses: the set chia pudding first, then the banana-miso mash, then the Greek yogurt.
4. Garnish with flaked almonds and a dusting of cacao powder.
