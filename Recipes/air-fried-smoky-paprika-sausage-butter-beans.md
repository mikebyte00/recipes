  - { ingredient: vegetarian-sausage, qty: 4, unit: each, prep: sliced into chunks }
  - { ingredient: butter-beans, qty: 300, unit: g, prep: "drained, patted dry" }
  - { ingredient: oil-spray, qty: 4, unit: g, staple: true }
  - { ingredient: smoked-paprika, qty: 3, unit: g, staple: true }
  - { ingredient: cottage-cheese, qty: 160, unit: g, note: to serve }
---

1. Toss the sliced sausages with oil spray and smoked paprika in a bowl.
2. Tip into your air fryer basket and air fry at 200°C for 10–12 minutes, adding the drained butter beans halfway through, until the sausages are browned and the beans are crispy on the outside.
3. **To Serve:** Spoon into bowls and fold through cottage cheese for a creamy, smoky finish.
