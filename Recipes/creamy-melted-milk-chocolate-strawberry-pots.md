1. **Melt the chocolate:** Break the milk chocolate into a small microwave-safe bowl. Heat in short 10-second bursts, stirring between each burst until completely melted and smooth.
2. **Temper the yogurt:** Add 2 tablespoons of room-temperature yogurt straight into the melted chocolate and whisk rapidly. (This tempers the warm chocolate so it doesn't seize or clump).
3. **Combine:** Fold the smooth chocolate-yogurt mixture into the remaining yogurt, honey, and vanilla extract until silky and evenly blended.
4. **Assemble:** Divide half of the sliced strawberries between two bowls, spoon the melted milk chocolate yogurt cream over the top, and finish with the remaining fresh strawberries. Chill for 15–20 minutes before serving.
