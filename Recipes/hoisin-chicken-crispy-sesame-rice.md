1. Cook the jasmine rice in the rice cooker.
2. Heat the air fryer to 200°C. Put the chicken fillets in one side of the air fryer (or on the air fryer rack) and cook for 10 minutes. Meanwhile, mix the rice in a bowl with the soy sauce, 4 tsp of the vinegar and most of the sesame seeds.
3. Put the rice in the other side of the air fryer (or under the rack) and pat it down into a layer. Cook everything for a further 8 minutes.
4. Meanwhile, warm the hoisin sauce and the remaining 2 tsp vinegar in a small pan over a low heat for about a minute. Toss the hot, crispy chicken in the sauce and serve with the salad onions, cucumber and remaining sesame seeds.
