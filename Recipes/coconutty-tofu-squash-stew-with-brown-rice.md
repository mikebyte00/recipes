1. Add the brown rice and water to the rice cooker and run a standard cycle.
2. Toss the tofu in a bowl with ½ tbsp of the oil, the salt and a good grind of black pepper. Fry in the skillet over a medium-high heat for 10 minutes, turning every few minutes, until golden on each side. Lift out and set aside.
3. Turn up the heat. Add the remaining ¾ tbsp oil with the onion, squash, garlic, peppercorns and bay leaf (if using). Fry for 10–12 minutes, stirring regularly, until soft.
4. Add the vinegar, soy sauce, sugar and coconut milk and simmer very gently for 10–12 minutes. Return the tofu to the pan and simmer for a final 10 minutes, until coated in a syrupy sauce (add a splash of water to loosen if needed). Discard the peppercorns and bay leaf.
5. Serve with the brown rice, and the chopped chilli and coriander if you like.
