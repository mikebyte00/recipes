1. Boil the wholewheat spaghetti in salted water until al dente, dropping the peas in for the final 2 minutes. Drain, reserving a mugful of the pasta water.
2. Meanwhile, fry the chopped pancetta in the olive oil over a medium heat until the fat has rendered and the edges are crisp, about 5 minutes. Take the pan off the heat.
3. Beat the eggs with three-quarters of the grated parmesan and a generous amount of black pepper until it forms a thick paste. Season with only a pinch of salt — the pancetta and parmesan are already salty.
4. Tip the drained spaghetti and peas into the pancetta pan **off the heat** and toss through the rendered fat. Pour in the egg mixture and stir hard and constantly, loosening with splashes of pasta water until the sauce turns glossy and coats every strand. Keeping the pan off direct heat is what stops the egg scrambling.
5. **To Serve:** Plate immediately onto warm plates, finished with the last of the parmesan and more black pepper.
