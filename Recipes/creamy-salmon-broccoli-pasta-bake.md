1. Defrost the salmon in the fridge overnight. Heat the oven to 200°C fan. Cook the penne in a pot of salted water as per the pack instructions, adding the broccoli for the last 3 minutes. Keep a mugful of the water, then drain.
2. Back in the pot, stir the cream cheese, garlic, half the parmesan, the lemon zest, plenty of black pepper and a splash of the pasta water into the pasta to make a loose, creamy sauce. Tip it into an ovenproof dish.
3. Nestle the salmon on top, skin-side down, so the flesh is exposed. Season, squeeze over half the lemon and scatter the rest of the parmesan over the pasta. Bake for 15–18 minutes, until the salmon flakes easily and the pasta is bubbling at the edges.
4. Serve one fillet per person on their pasta with the rest of the lemon in wedges. Flake the salmon into the pasta on your plate and leave the skin behind.
