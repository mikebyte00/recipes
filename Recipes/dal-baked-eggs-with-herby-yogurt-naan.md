1. Preheat the oven to 190°C (170°C fan). Heat the oil or ghee in a small ovenproof frying pan until hot. Add the spices from the smallest pouch in the spice kit, then take the pan off the heat for 15 seconds so the spices don't burn. Return the pan to the heat and stir in the lentils from the large pouch.
2. Add the tarka from the medium pouch, 2 tbsp water and the spinach. Cover with a lid (or a baking sheet) for a couple of minutes to wilt the spinach. Stir in the chickpeas and bring to a simmer.
3. Make 2 wells in the dal, then break an egg into each. Bake for 10–12 minutes, or until the whites are set and the yolks are done to your liking. Put the naans on a baking tray, sprinkle with a little water and bake for the final 2–3 minutes.
4. While the eggs cook, roughly chop the coriander leaves and stir into the yogurt with the lime juice, then season. Serve the baked eggs and dal topped with dollops of the lime and coriander yogurt, with the naan and some extra coriander.
5. Simple swap, to skip the oven: at step 3, cover the pan of eggs with a lid and cook over a low heat for 6–8 minutes instead, and heat the naans in the toaster (without the water).
