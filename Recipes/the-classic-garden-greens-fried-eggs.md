1. Toast the bread.
2. Mash the avocado.
3. Sauté the spinach and sliced spring onions in a skillet until wilted.
4. Push them to the side to fry your eggs.
5. **To Serve:** Serve over toasted sourdough with the avocado sliced or mashed on top.
