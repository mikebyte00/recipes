1. Cut the potatoes into thick chips. Soak them in cold water for 10 minutes to remove excess starch, then drain and dry them thoroughly with a clean tea towel.
2. Toss the dry chips with the olive oil, sea salt, black pepper, and garlic powder. Air-fry at 200°C for 22–25 minutes, shaking the basket a few times.
3. Add the dusted fillets and the frozen goujons straight into the air-fryer basket on top of the chips for the final 12–14 minutes of cooking. Everything will crisp up at the same time.
4. **To Serve:** Give each person one large dusted sole fillet and split the crispy goujons evenly between both plates. Add a portion of golden skin-on chips, a pile of fresh mixed salad, and a generous dollop of tartare sauce with a lemon wedge.
