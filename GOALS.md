## Nutrient Goals

- **Protein: ~130g/day, as a floor.** Overshooting costs nothing — carbohydrates
  and fat are untracked, so there is no budget for extra protein to blow.
- **Calories: ~1800/day, as a ceiling.** Being under is better than being over.

Both are rough objectives. A day that misses one is not a failure to correct;
these steer generation, they do not gate it.

## Macro Bands

The per-Slot budget a new Recipe aims at. **Target** is the figure to hit;
**range** is what the existing 37 Recipes actually span, and is guidance, not a
gate. Figures are per person — every Recipe serves 2.

| Slot | Target protein | Target kcal | Protein range | kcal range |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Breakfast | ~27g | ~420 | 17–32g | 295–490 |
| Lunch | ~45g | ~380 | 43–48g | 330–420 |
| Dinner | ~48g | ~730 | 42–58g | 695–800 |
| Pudding | ~12g | ~155 | 10–20g | 100–250 |
| **Day** | **~132g** | **~1685** | | |

The four targets sum to a day that clears the protein floor and sits under the
kcal ceiling with headroom.

**Bands are checked at the day, not at the Recipe.** A day assembled entirely
from the bottom of each range comes to ~113g, and one from the top comes to
~1880 kcal — so range-legal does not mean goal-compliant. Sum the day.

**Breakfast splits by protein type.** The two clusters do not overlap: egg
breakfasts run 17–27g, sausage breakfasts 29–32g. Aim an egg breakfast at the
top of its cluster, since it is the harder one to make protein-dense.

**Pudding's range is widened, not measured.** All four existing puddings sit in
11–13.5g / 120–170 kcal, but they are four variations on one idea and that
sample is too small to bound the slot. The range above is deliberately loose.
