---
title: Zero-Prep Sausage & Chickpea Crisp Hash
slot: breakfast
protein: sausage
tags: [quick, bulk-cook]
appliances: [air-fryer]
macros: { protein_g: 32, kcal: 385 }
ingredients:
  - { ingredient: vegetarian-sausage, qty: 4,   unit: each, prep: sliced into chunks }
  - { ingredient: chickpeas,          qty: 1,   unit: can,  prep: drained, patted dry }
  - { ingredient: cottage-cheese,     qty: 160, unit: g,    note: to serve }
---

1. Slice the sausages into chunks.
2. Toss them and the chickpeas into the air fryer basket with a light oil
   spray, salt and pepper.
3. Air fry at 200°C for 10–12 minutes, shaking halfway, until the sausages are
   golden and the chickpeas are crispy.
4. **To serve:** tip into a bowl and stir through the cottage cheese.
