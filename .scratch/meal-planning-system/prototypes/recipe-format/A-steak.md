1. Put the raw rice, sliced mushrooms, a pinch of salt and water straight into
   the rice cooker. The mushrooms steam into the grains, releasing their juices.
   When done, stir through the parmesan and half the butter.
2. Sear the steak on the Ninja Sizzle using the remaining butter.
3. Serve the steak over a mountain of the mushroom pilaf.
