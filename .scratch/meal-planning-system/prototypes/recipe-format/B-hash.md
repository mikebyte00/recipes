# Zero-Prep Sausage & Chickpea Crisp Hash

*The Pan: One Air Fryer Basket*

**Ingredients:**
- 4 vegetarian sausages, sliced into chunks
- 1 can chickpeas, drained and patted dry
- 160g cottage cheese (to serve)

**How to cook:**
1. Slice the sausages into chunks.
2. Toss them and the chickpeas into the air fryer basket with a light oil
   spray, salt and pepper.
3. Air fry at 200°C for 10–12 minutes, shaking halfway.
4. **To serve:** tip into a bowl and stir through the cottage cheese.
