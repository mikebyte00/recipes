1. Melt the white chocolate in 10-second bursts.
2. Whisk 3 tablespoons of the yogurt into the melted chocolate to temper it,
   then fold that back into the remaining yogurt with the vanilla.
3. Whisk vigorously for 60 seconds to incorporate air.
4. Fold in the raspberries and chill for 30 minutes to let the cocoa butter set.
