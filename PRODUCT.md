# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

A household of two. Both use the browse page (`index.html`) on their phones
when they're out, such as when deciding or checking a list, and on a tablet in
the kitchen while cooking.

## Product Purpose

A personal meal-planning system: a pool of Recipes and a weekly Plan built from
them, which produces shopping output. That output is a Waitrose Multi-search
block plus counter lists for Soutars and Dorset Meats. The problem it solves is
time. Assembling the weekly order is the expensive part, not deciding what to
eat. Success means a week planned and its order out quickly, and Recipes that
are easy to cook from.

Planning the week and cooking from a Recipe matter equally.

## Positioning

It is built around one household's real week. Every Plan is built Slot by Slot
for a specific calendar week, every ingredient resolves to a Pin (a product the
household has already bought and vetted), and the shopping output splits by the
Stores they actually use. A generic recipe app can't copy that.

## Operating Context

- Weekly ritual: harvest last week's order into Pins, build the Plan in the
  page's Plan mode, generate the shopping list, then paste into Waitrose or fill
  the trolley by line number. Checkout and payment stay with the user.
- Cooking happens at the counter, with the page on a tablet.
- A fourth table of household reminders (from `HOUSEHOLD.md`) gets ticked
  through each week.
- Vocabulary is fixed in `CONTEXT.md`: Recipe, Slot, Plan, Fakeaway, Macros,
  Band, Pin, Store.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- `index.html` is generated by `bin/browse.py`, committed, and served by GitHub
  Pages from the branch root. The committed page is the live product.
- There is no framework. Only two pieces of code exist (`bin/browse.py` and
  `bin/shopping-list.py`), and a third is a decision that needs a ticket.
- Serves 2, always. Only protein and kcal are tracked. Every macro shown carries
  `~`.
- The repo is public, so no personal order data is ever written to it.

## Evidence on Hand

Real content: 37+ Recipes in `Recipes/`, Plans in `Plans/`, `PINS.md`,
`GOALS.md`, and `HOUSEHOLD.md`. There are no testimonials or outside users, and
none should be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Time saved is the measure. Every screen should shorten the weekly order or
   the cook.
2. Be honest about estimates. Macros are approximations and are shown as such.
3. The page and the repo agree. A change isn't done until the published page
   shows it.
4. Plain over impressive. Simple Recipes and a simple interface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard good practice, plus one product-specific need: the cooking view has to
read at arm's length on a tablet on the counter, with messy hands, so it needs
large type and large tap targets.
