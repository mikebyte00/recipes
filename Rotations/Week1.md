# Week One

## Monday

### Breakfast — Zero-Prep Sausage & Chickpea Crisp Hash
*The Pan: One Air Fryer Basket*

**Ingredients (For 2 People):**
- 4 vegan sausages, sliced into chunks
- 1 can drained chickpeas (patted dry)
- 160g cottage cheese (to serve)

**How to cook:**
1. Slice vegan sausages into chunks.
2. Toss them and drained chickpeas (patted dry) directly into your air fryer basket with a light oil spray, salt, and pepper.
3. Air fry at 200°C for 10–12 minutes, shaking halfway, until the sausages are golden and the chickpeas are beautifully crispy.
4. **To Serve:** Tip into a bowl and stir through cottage cheese.

**Nutrient Profile (Per Serving):** ~32g Protein | ~385 kcal

### Lunch — Warm Tofu, Chickpea & Feta Salad Pan
*The Pan: One Skillet*

**Ingredients (For 2 People):**
- 1 pack Tofu
- 1 can drained chickpeas
- 150g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 20g lemon juice
- 60g lighter feta

**How to cook:**
1. Cut the tofu into pieces.
2. Fry tofu until cooked through.
3. Toss drained chickpeas and halved cherry tomatoes straight into the hot pan with the tofu.
4. Cook just for 2 minutes until the tomatoes begin to soften and release their juices.
5. Toss with lemon juice, pull off the heat, and crumble over lighter feta.

**Nutrient Profile (Per Serving):** ~44g Protein | ~360 kcal

### Dinner — Pan-Seared White Fish with Rice-Cooker Lemon-Herb Grains & Parmesan Asparagus
*The Pan: Rice Cooker & Ninja Sizzle*

**Ingredients (For 2 People):**
- 2 fillets white fish
- 75g wholegrain rice
- 1 pack pre-cooked quinoa
- 250g asparagus
- 60g parmesan, grated
- 20g fresh lemon juice (approx. 2 squeezes)
- 4g dried herbs (approx. 2 pinches)
- Pinch of salt

**How to cook:**
1. Place the rice into the rice cooker with water (1:2), a squeeze of fresh lemon juice, and a pinch of dried herbs, and let it run.
2. Toss the raw asparagus spears onto the Ninja Sizzle for 5 minutes until charred, then shower them with the grated parmesan right on the grill so it forms a crispy cheese crust.
3. Cook the white fish on the sizzle plate for 2–3 minutes each side until it easily flakes.

**Nutrient Profile (Per Serving):** ~700 kcal | ~48g Protein

### Pudding — The Real Salted Peanut Butter Mousse

**Ingredients (For 2 People):**
- 200g Fage 2% yogurt
- 20g real peanut butter (100% nut)
- 10g honey
- Pinch sea salt flakes

**Method:**
1. In a small bowl, whisk the peanut butter and honey together until smooth. If it's too thick, add a teaspoon of warm water.
2. In a large bowl, whisk the Greek yogurt until light and airy.
3. Fold the peanut butter mixture into the yogurt.
4. Serve topped with a generous sprinkle of sea salt flakes to make the peanut butter flavor "pop."

**Nutrient Profile (Per Serving):** ~165 kcal | ~13.5g Protein

**Daily Nutrient Profile (Individual):** ~146.7g Protein | ~1689 kcal

## Tuesday

### Breakfast — Mediterranean Feta & Tomato Egg Scramble
*The Pan: One Skillet*

**Ingredients (For 2 People):**
- 60g lighter feta, crumbled
- 4 eggs
- 150g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 100g baby spinach
- 2 slices seeded sourdough

**How to cook:**
1. Toast your sourdough.
2. Whisk eggs in a bowl.

4. Pour over the whisked eggs and scramble gently. Just before taking off the heat, fold through crumbled feta so it melts slightly, then pile high onto toast.

**Nutrient Profile (Per Serving):** ~27g Protein | ~415 kcal

### Lunch — Creamy Pesto Chicken & Cannellini Beans
*The Pan: One Skillet*

**Ingredients (For 2 People):**
- 300g chicken breast, diced
- 1 can drained cannellini beans
- 150g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 30g green pesto
- 120g cottage cheese

**How to cook:**
1. Dice chicken breast and fry in a hot skillet with olive oil for 5–6 minutes until golden and cooked through.
2. Pour in drained cannellini beans, halved cherry tomatoes, and green pesto.
3. Toss for 2 minutes until hot, then remove from the heat and stir in cottage cheese for a rich, velvety sauce.

**Nutrient Profile (Per Serving):** ~48g Protein | ~410 kcal

### Dinner — Korean Gochujang Glazed Chicken with Sesame Sugar Snap Peas & Pearl Barley
*The Pan: Rice Cooker, Skillet & Ninja Sizzle Plate*

**Ingredients (For 2 People):**
- 2 chicken breasts
- 150g raw pearl barley
- 150g sugar snap peas
- 25g gochujang (Korean chili paste)
- 15g honey
- 15g soy sauce or tamari
- 10g sesame oil
- Pinch of salt

**How to cook:**
1. Add raw pearl barley, a pinch of salt, and water (1:2 ratio) into the rice cooker. Run on a standard grain or brown rice cycle until plump, chewy, and tender.
2. Whisk the gochujang, honey, soy sauce, and half the sesame oil into a thick glaze.
3. Sear the chicken breast on the hob. In the last 2 minutes of cooking, pour the gochujang glaze over the chicken so it bubbles and caramelizes into a sticky coating.
4. Toss the sugar snap peas onto the sizzle plate with the remaining sesame oil for 2 minutes until bright and tender-crisp. Serve hot over the savory pearl barley.

**Nutrient Profile (Per Serving):** ~715 kcal | ~47g Protein

### Pudding — Zesty Lemon & Vanilla Bean "Cheesecake"

**Ingredients (For 2 People):**
- 200g Fage 2% yogurt
- 12g honey
- 80g raspberries
- ½ fresh lemon, juiced
- Zest of ½ lemon
- 2g vanilla extract

**Method:**
1. Whisk the Fage 2%, honey, and vanilla extract until very smooth.
2. Stir in the lemon juice and half of the lemon zest. The acidity will naturally thicken the Greek yogurt.
3. Divide into bowls and top with raspberries and the remaining lemon zest.
4. Chill for 20 minutes before serving.

**Nutrient Profile (Per Serving):** ~120 kcal | ~11g Protein

**Daily Nutrient Profile (Individual):** ~142.4g Protein | ~1744 kcal

### Dinner — Creamy Tarragon & Dijon Salmon with Air-Fried Herb Baby Potatoes & Charred Green Beans
*The Pan: Air Fryer, Ninja Sizzle Plate and Skillet*

**Ingredients (For 2 People):**
- 2 salmon fillets
- 350g raw baby potatoes (halved, skins on)
- 200g fine green beans (trimmed)
- 60g light cream cheese (e.g., Philadelphia Light)
- 100ml vegetable stock (or splash of dry white wine + stock)
- 15g Dijon mustard (approx. 1 tbsp)
- 10g olive oil (approx. 2 teaspoons)
- 10g fresh tarragon (finely chopped)
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- Pinch of sea salt, black pepper & dried parsley

**How to cook:**
1. Toss halved baby potatoes with 5g olive oil, sea salt, and dried parsley. Air-fry at 200°C for 18 minutes until golden and crispy-edged.
2. Heat remaining oil on the Ninja Sizzle plate. Char green beans for 4 minutes, then push to the side. Sear salmon fillets skin-side down for 4 minutes, flip for 2 minutes, then remove fish and beans to a plate.
3. Add minced garlic to the sizzle plate for 30 seconds. Pour in the stock/wine and Dijon mustard, letting it bubble for 1 minute. Whisk in the light cream cheese and fresh tarragon until smooth and creamy.
4. **To Serve:** Plate the crispy air-fried potatoes alongside the charred green beans, lay the seared salmon on top, and spoon the warm tarragon-Dijon cream sauce over everything.

**Nutrient Profile (Per Serving):** ~710 kcal | ~47g Protein

### Pudding — Creamy Melted Milk Chocolate & Strawberry Pots

**Ingredients (For 2 People):**
- 200g Fage 2% yogurt
- 8g honey
- 120g fresh strawberries
- 24g milk chocolate
- 4g vanilla extract

**Method:**
1. **Melt the chocolate:** Break 24g of milk chocolate into a small microwave-safe bowl. Heat in short 10-second bursts, stirring between each burst until completely melted and smooth.
2. **Temper the yogurt:** Add 2 tablespoons of room-temperature Fage 2% Greek yogurt straight into the melted chocolate and whisk rapidly. (This tempers the warm chocolate so it doesn't seize or clump).
3. **Combine:** Fold the smooth chocolate-yogurt mixture into the remaining Greek yogurt, 8g honey, and 4g vanilla extract until silky and evenly blended.
4. **Assemble:** Divide half of the sliced strawberries between two bowls, spoon the melted milk chocolate yogurt cream over the top, and finish with the remaining fresh strawberries. Chill for 15–20 minutes before serving.

**Nutrient Profile (Per Serving):** ~160 kcal | ~11g Protein

**Daily Nutrient Profile (Individual):** ~139.5g Protein | ~1724 kcal

### Dinner — Sizzled Sirloin Steak with Rice-Cooker Mushroom & Truffle Grain Pilaf
*The Pan: Rice Cooker & Ninja Sizzle*

**Ingredients (For 2 People):**
- 300g sirloin steak
- 150g raw brown rice
- 200g chestnut mushrooms, sliced
- 50g parmesan, grated

**How to cook:**
1. Pop the raw rice or quinoa, the sliced raw mushrooms, a pinch of salt, and water straight into the rice cooker. The mushrooms will steam beautifully into the grains, releasing their rich juices. When done, stir through the grated parmesan and half the butter.

3. Serve the steak over a mountain of the earthy, cheesy mushroom pilaf.

**Nutrient Profile (Per Serving):** ~760 kcal | ~46g Protein

### Pudding — The Raspberries & White Chocolate Ganache

**Ingredients (For 2 People):**
- 200g Fage 2% yogurt
- 24g high-quality white chocolate (e.g., Lindt)
- 80g fresh raspberries
- 4g vanilla bean paste

**Method:**
1. Melt the white chocolate carefully in 10-second bursts.
2. Whisk 3 tablespoons of Greek yogurt into the melted chocolate to temper it, then fold this mixture into the remaining yogurt and vanilla.
3. Whisk the entire mixture vigorously for 60 seconds to incorporate air.
4. Gently fold in the raspberries and chill for at least 30 minutes to let the cocoa butter set the mousse.

**Nutrient Profile (Per Serving):** ~170 kcal | ~11g Protein

**Daily Nutrient Profile (Individual):** ~128.4g Protein | ~1822 kcal

### Lunch — Air-Fried Harissa Tofu & Chickpea Crisp
*The Pan: One Air Fryer Basket*

**Ingredients (For 2 People):**
- 1 pack tofu, chopped into bite-sized pieces
- 1 can drained chickpeas (patted dry)
- 30g harissa paste

**Nutrient Profile (Per Serving):** ~46g Protein | ~390 kcal

### Dinner — The "Fakeaway" Peri-Peri Chicken Burger
*The Pan: Ninja Air Fryer, Toaster & Sizzle Plate*

**Ingredients (Serves 2):**
- 2 small chicken breasts (pounded to an even thickness, about 150g–170g each)
- 2 tbsp Peri-Peri sauce (plus extra for glazing)
- 1 tsp olive oil
- 2 high-quality brioche or wholemeal burger buns
- Crisp lettuce
- Halloumi
- Pineapple
- ¼ red onion (thinly sliced)
- Garlic mayo
- Peri-Peri mayo
- Waffle fries
- Smoked paprika

**How to cook:**
1. Marinate the chicken breasts in the Peri-Peri sauce and olive oil for at least 30 minutes.
2. Place the chicken breasts and waffle fries in the air fryer. Cook at 200°C for 15–18 minutes. Brush the chicken with extra Peri-Peri sauce during the last 3 minutes of cooking. (Add the cheese slice to the chicken for the final 60 seconds if desired.)
3. Lightly toast your buns in the air fryer or a toaster.
4. Lightly grill halloumi and pineapple on the Ninja Sizzle.
5. Spread the spicy/garlic mayo on each half of the bun. Layer with lettuce, halloumi/pineapple, red onion, and the peri-peri chicken breast.
6. **To Serve:** Serve immediately with the side of spiced waffle fries.

**Nutrient Profile (Per Serving):** ~800 kcal | ~58g Protein

**Daily Nutrient Profile (Individual):** ~135g Protein | ~1565 kcal

### Lunch — Air-Fried Crispy Buffalo & Blue Cheese Butter Beans
*The Pan: One Air Fryer Basket + One Bowl*

**Ingredients (For 2 People):**
- 2 chicken breasts, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 1 can butter beans
- 30g Frank's RedHot
- 40g crumbled Saint Agur light stirred into 80g Greek yogurt
- 100g celery, finely diced (for crunch)

**How to cook:**
1. Toss chicken directly in a bowl with the buffalo sauce and light oil spray.
2. Tip into the air fryer basket and cook at 190°C for 12–14 minutes, shaking halfway, until crispy and cooked through.

### Dinner — Sicilian-Style Red Pesto White Fish over Wholewheat Spaghetti with Capers & Olives
*The Pan: Pot & Ninja Sizzle*

**Ingredients (For 2 People):**
- 2 white fish fillets
- 130g raw wholewheat spaghetti
- 40g red pesto (Pesto alla Siciliana)
- 30g black olives, halved
- 15g capers, drained
- 150g cherry tomatoes, halved

**How to cook:**
1. Boil your wholewheat spaghetti in salted water until tender, then drain.
2. Toss the halved cherry tomatoes, olives, and capers onto your Ninja Sizzle plate with the olive oil. Grill them for about 3 minutes until the tomatoes start to blister and soften.
3. Push the veggies to the side of the sizzle plate to make room, and place your white fish fillets directly onto the grill. Cook the fish for 3 minutes per side until the edges are golden and it flakes effortlessly with a fork.
4. In a large bowl or directly back in the pasta pan, toss your hot, drained spaghetti with the red pesto until fully coated.
5. **To Serve:** Plate up the vibrant red pesto pasta, then layer your perfectly seared fish and all those blistered, tangy tomatoes, olives, and capers directly on top.

**Nutrient Profile (Per Serving):** ~695 kcal | ~49g Protein
