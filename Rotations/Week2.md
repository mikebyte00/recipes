# Week Two

## Monday

### Breakfast — Air-Fried Smoky Paprika Sausage & Butter Beans
*The Pan: One Air Fryer Basket*

**Ingredients (For 2 People):**
- 4 vegan sausages, sliced into chunks
- 300g drained butter beans (patted dry)
- 4g oil spray (approx. 2 light sprays)
- 3g smoked paprika (approx. 1 teaspoon)
- 160g cottage cheese (to serve)

**How to cook:**
1. Toss sliced vegan sausages with oil spray and smoked paprika in a bowl.
2. Tip into your air fryer basket and air fry at 200°C for 10–12 minutes, add the drained butter beans halfway through, cook until the sausages are browned and the beans are crispy on the outside.
3. **To Serve:** Spoon into bowls and fold through cottage cheese for a creamy, smoky finish.

**Nutrient Profile (Per Serving):** ~30g Protein | ~380 kcal

### Lunch — Warm Tofu, Chickpea & Feta Salad Pan
*The Pan: One Skillet*

**Ingredients (For 2 People):**
- 1 pack tofu
- 1 can drained chickpeas
- 150g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 20g lemon juice
- 60g lighter feta

**How to cook:**
1. Cut the tofu into pieces.
2. Fry tofu until cooked through.
3. Toss drained chickpeas and halved cherry tomatoes straight into the hot pan with the tofu.
4. Cook just for 2 minutes until the tomatoes begin to soften and release their juices.
5. Toss with lemon juice, pull off the heat, and crumble over lighter feta.

**Nutrient Profile (Per Serving):** ~44g Protein | ~360 kcal

### Dinner — Sticky Miso-Glazed Fish with Ginger-Steamed Greens & Rice/Quinoa Blend
*The Pan: Rice Cooker & Air Fryer*

**Ingredients (For 2 People):**
- 2 white fish fillets
- 130g rice
- 150g pak choi (bok choy), halved
- 20g white miso paste
- 15g honey
- 10g soy sauce
- 10g sesame oil
- 5g fresh grated ginger

**How to cook:**
1. Add the wholegrain rice into the rice cooker with water (1:2 ratio), grated ginger, and a pinch of salt.
2. Whisk miso paste, honey, soy sauce, and sesame oil together. Coat your fish fillets generously.
3. Preheat the sizzle plate and char the pak choi.
4. Air-fry the fish at 190°C for 8–10 minutes until the miso glaze is bubbling and golden. Serve directly over the ginger-scented grain and greens base with lime.

**Nutrient Profile (Per Serving):** ~705 kcal | ~46g Protein

### Pudding — The Real Salted Peanut Butter Mousse

**Ingredients (For 2 People):**
- 200g Fage 2% yogurt
- 20g real peanut butter (100% nut)
- 10g honey
- Pinch sea salt flakes

**Method:**
1. In a small bowl, whisk the peanut butter and honey together until smooth. If it's too thick, add a teaspoon of warm water.
2. In a large bowl, whisk the Greek yogurt until light and airy.
3. Fold the peanut butter mixture into the yogurt.
4. Serve topped with a generous sprinkle of sea salt flakes to make the peanut butter flavor "pop."

**Nutrient Profile (Per Serving):** ~165 kcal | ~13.5g Protein

**Daily Nutrient Profile (Individual):** ~141.7g Protein | ~1659 kcal

## Tuesday

### Breakfast — Savory Egg, Avocado & Homemade Baked Bean Skillet
*The Pan: One Skillet*

**Ingredients (For 2 People):**
- 4 eggs
- 1 whole avocado
- 2 slices seeded sourdough
- 150g cannellini or haricot beans (drained)
- 15g tomato purée (approx. 1 tablespoon)
- 3g smoked paprika (approx. 1 teaspoon)
- 5g Worcestershire sauce (approx. 1 dash)
- 5g honey or maple syrup (approx. 1 teaspoon)
- 15g water (approx. 1 splash)

**How to cook:**
1. Warm drained beans in the corner of your skillet with tomato purée, smoked paprika, Worcestershire sauce, honey, and a splash of water for 2 minutes until thick and glossy.
2. Toast your bread.
3. Mash the avocado and spread on top of the bread.
4. Fry your eggs alongside.
5. **To Serve:** Serve over toasted sourdough with mashed avocado.

**Nutrient Profile (Per Serving):** ~25g Protein | ~490 kcal

### Lunch — Creamy Mild Curry Tofu & Butter Beans
*The Pan: One Skillet*

**Ingredients (For 2 People):**
- 1 pack tofu
- 10g olive oil (approx. 2 splashes)
- 8g mild curry powder (approx. 1 tablespoon)
- 240g drained butter beans
- 100g baby spinach
- 120g 0% Greek yogurt (or Waitrose lighter cottage cheese)

**How to cook:**
1. Fry chicken mini fillets in a hot skillet with olive oil and curry powder for 5–6 minutes until cooked through and coated in spices.
2. Pour in drained butter beans and baby spinach with a small splash of water. Toss for 2 minutes until the spinach wilts and the beans are hot.
3. Remove from the heat completely and stir through Greek yogurt to create a creamy sauce.

**Nutrient Profile (Per Serving):** ~45g Protein | ~420 kcal

### Dinner — Moroccan Spiced Chicken with Air-Fried Butternut Squash & Flaked Almonds
*The Pan: Air Fryer*

**Ingredients (For 2 People):**
- 4 chicken thighs (skin on, bone out)
- 450g diced butternut squash
- 150g courgette, sliced into half-moons
- 15g Ras el Hanout or Moroccan spice blend
- 15g flaked almonds

**How to cook:**
1. Toss the frozen butternut squash cubes into the air fryer and slice courgette in a bowl with olive oil, salt, and half the Moroccan spice blend.
2. Tip into the air fryer and cook at 200°C for 15 minutes, tossing halfway through, until the squash is tender and caramelized at the edges. Scatter the flaked almonds in for the final 2 minutes to toast.
3. Rub the remaining spice blend onto the chicken thighs and cook in the air fryer for 12 minutes. Serve over the vibrant, nutty roasted squash base.

**Total Plate Profile (Per Serving):** ~758 kcal | ~75.5g Protein

### Pudding — Zesty Lemon & Vanilla Bean "Cheesecake"

**Ingredients (For 2 People):**
- 200g Fage 2% yogurt
- 12g honey
- 80g raspberries
- ½ fresh lemon, juiced
- Zest of ½ lemon
- 2g vanilla extract

**Method:**
1. Whisk the Fage 2%, honey, and vanilla extract until very smooth.
2. Stir in the lemon juice and half of the lemon zest. The acidity will naturally thicken the Greek yogurt.
3. Divide into bowls and top with raspberries and the remaining lemon zest.
4. Chill for 20 minutes before serving.

**Nutrient Profile (Per Serving):** ~120 kcal | ~11g Protein

**Daily Nutrient Profile (Individual):** ~168.9g Protein | ~1862 kcal

### Dinner — Creamy Florentine Salmon over Wholewheat Penne with Blistered Tomatoes & Spinach
*The Pan: Pot, Ninja Sizzle Plate and Skillet*

**Ingredients (For 2 People):**
- 300g salmon fillets (skin-on, 2 x 150g)
- 130g raw wholewheat penne
- 150g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 100g fresh baby spinach
- 150g passata
- 50g light cream cheese (or Quark)
- 20g parmesan, finely grated
- 10g olive oil (approx. 2 teaspoons)
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- Pinch of sea salt, cracked black pepper & dried oregano

**How to cook:**
1. Boil wholewheat penne in salted water until al dente, then drain (reserving 3 tbsp pasta water).
2. Sear salmon fillets in 5g oil on the Ninja Sizzle (4 mins skin-side, 2 mins flip). Toss the halved tomatoes onto the plate alongside for the final 2 minutes so they blister. Remove salmon and tomatoes.
3. Sauté garlic in the oil for 1 minute. Add passata and oregano, simmering for 2 minutes. Stir in the cream cheese and grated parmesan until a smooth, rosy cream sauce forms. Drop in the spinach to wilt.
4. Toss the hot pasta directly into the creamy sauce with a splash of reserved pasta water.
5. **To Serve:** Serve topped with the blistered tomatoes and seared salmon.

**Nutrient Profile (Per Serving):** ~710 kcal | ~49g Protein

### Pudding — Creamy Melted Milk Chocolate & Strawberry Pots

**Ingredients (For 2 People):**
- 200g Fage 2% yogurt
- 8g honey
- 120g fresh strawberries
- 24g milk chocolate
- 4g vanilla extract

**Method:**
1. **Melt the chocolate:** Break 24g of milk chocolate into a small microwave-safe bowl. Heat in short 10-second bursts, stirring between each burst until completely melted and smooth.
2. **Temper the yogurt:** Add 2 tablespoons of room-temperature Fage 2% Greek yogurt straight into the melted chocolate and whisk rapidly. (This tempers the warm chocolate so it doesn't seize or clump).
3. **Combine:** Fold the smooth chocolate-yogurt mixture into the remaining Greek yogurt, 8g honey, and 4g vanilla extract until silky and evenly blended.
4. **Assemble:** Divide half of the sliced strawberries between two bowls, spoon the melted milk chocolate yogurt cream over the top, and finish with the remaining fresh strawberries. Chill for 15–20 minutes before serving.

**Nutrient Profile (Per Serving):** ~160 kcal | ~11g Protein

**Daily Nutrient Profile (Individual):** ~133.5g Protein | ~1744 kcal

### Dinner — Steak Tagliata with Herb-Roasted New Potatoes & Peppery Rocket-Pine Nut Salad
*The Pan: Ninja Air Fryer & Ninja Sizzle*

**Ingredients (For 2 People):**
- 2 sirloin steaks
- 400g raw new potatoes or baby potatoes, halved
- 60g fresh rocket (arugula)
- 15g pine nuts
- 20g parmesan flakes
- 15g olive oil (approx. 3 teaspoons)
- 10g balsamic vinegar
- Pinch of coarse sea salt & rosemary

**How to cook:**
1. Toss the halved raw baby potatoes (skins on) with 10g of olive oil, sea salt, and a pinch of rosemary. Air-fry at 200°C for 18–20 minutes until ultra-crisp.
2. Sear your steak to your liking on the Ninja Sizzle, then let it rest for 3 minutes before carving into thin strips.
3. Toss the rocket with the remaining olive oil and balsamic vinegar. Plate the hot crispy potatoes, arrange the sliced steak over the rocket salad, and finish with a scatter of pine nuts and shaved parmesan.

**Nutrient Profile (Per Serving):** ~720 kcal | ~46g Protein

### Pudding — The Raspberries & White Chocolate Ganache

**Ingredients (For 2 People):**
- 200g Fage 2% yogurt
- 24g high-quality white chocolate (e.g., Lindt)
- 80g fresh raspberries
- 4g vanilla bean paste

**Method:**
1. Melt the white chocolate carefully in 10-second bursts.
2. Whisk 3 tablespoons of Greek yogurt into the melted chocolate to temper it, then fold this mixture into the remaining yogurt and vanilla.
3. Whisk the entire mixture vigorously for 60 seconds to incorporate air.
4. Gently fold in the raspberries and chill for at least 30 minutes to let the cocoa butter set the mousse.

**Nutrient Profile (Per Serving):** ~170 kcal | ~11g Protein

**Daily Nutrient Profile (Individual):** ~133.4g Protein | ~1747 kcal

### Dinner — The Combo Lemon Sole Plate & Air-Fryer Chips
*The Pan: Ninja Air Fryer*

**Ingredients (Total for 2 People):**
- 1 pack (270g) Waitrose 2 Lightly Dusted Lemon Sole Fillets
- Box Waitrose Frozen Breaded Atlantic Lemon Sole Goujons
- 500g potatoes, cut into thick chips (skins left on)
- Waitrose tartare sauce
- 1 large bag of mixed salad greens (plus cucumber and radishes)
- 1 lemon, cut into wedges
- Sea salt, black pepper, and a pinch of garlic powder

**How to cook:**
1. Cut the potatoes into thick chips. Soak them in cold water for 10 minutes to remove excess starch, then drain and dry them thoroughly with a clean tea towel.
2. Toss the dry chips with the 2 tsp of olive oil, sea salt, black pepper, and garlic powder. Air-fry at 200°C for 22–25 minutes, shaking the basket a few times.
3. Place your 2 lightly dusted fillets and your handful of frozen breaded goujons together on a baking tray lined with parchment paper. Pop them into the oven at 200°C fan for 14–16 minutes (or add them straight into the air-fryer basket on top of the chips for the final 12–14 minutes of cooking). Everything will crisp up beautifully at the exact same time.
4. **To Serve:** Give each person one large dusted sole fillet and split the crispy goujons evenly between both plates. Add your 250g portion of golden skin-on chips, a massive pile of fresh mixed salad, and a generous dollop of rich tartare sauce with a lemon wedge.

**Nutrient Profile (Per Serving):** ~800 kcal | ~42g Protein

**Daily Nutrient Profile (Individual):** ~118g Protein | ~1545 kcal

### Lunch — Creamy Gochujang & Sweetcorn White Bean Skillet
*The Pan: One Skillet*

**Ingredients (For 2 People):**
- 2 chicken breasts
- 10g olive oil
- 30g gochujang chili paste
- 1 can cannellini beans
- Canned sweetcorn, drained
- 120g cottage cheese

**How to cook:**
1. Tip the diced chicken straight into a hot skillet with the olive oil and gochujang paste. Stir-fry for 5 minutes until fully cooked and coated in the glossy paste.
2. Dump in the drained cannellini beans and sweetcorn. Toss for 1–2 minutes until steaming hot.
3. Remove from the heat completely and stir through the lighter cottage cheese to create a spicy, creamy, comforting sauce.

**Nutrient Profile (Per Serving):** ~47g Protein | ~410 kcal

### Dinner — Zesty Lemon-Herb White Fish over Wholewheat Linguine with Roasted Courgette & Pine Nuts
*The Pan: Ninja Sizzle, Pot and Skillet*

**Ingredients (For 2 People):**
- 2 white fish fillets
- 130g raw wholewheat linguine or spaghetti
- 200g courgette, sliced into thin half-moons
- 15g pine nuts
- 20g butter
- 10g olive oil (approx. 2 teaspoons)
- Juice and zest of 1 fresh lemon
- 4g dried oregano or Italian seasoning
- Pinch of salt

**How to cook:**
1. Boil the wholewheat linguine in salted water until tender, then drain (reserve a splash of pasta water).
2. Toss the courgette slices onto the Ninja Sizzle with the olive oil and a pinch of salt. Grill for 4–5 minutes until beautifully caramelized and tender. Toss the pine nuts onto the plate for the last 60 seconds to lightly toast, then set the veg and nuts aside.
3. Melt the butter on the hob with the lemon juice, half the lemon zest, and the dried herbs. Lay the white fish fillets directly into the lemon butter and cook for 2–3 minutes per side until it flakes effortlessly.
4. Toss the hot linguine, roasted courgette, and toasted pine nuts directly into the remaining lemon-butter juices on the plate (add a splash of pasta water if it needs loosening). Top with the flaky fish and finish with the remaining fresh lemon zest.

**Nutrient Profile (Per Serving):** ~705 kcal | ~48g Protein
